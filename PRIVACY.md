# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-19

Oura Personal Sync is a private, personal-use integration created to access and analyze the account owner's own Oura data.

## What data is accessed
- Oura account data explicitly authorized by the account owner
- Health and wellness metrics made available by the Oura API

## How data is used
- To generate personal daily summaries
- To support private analytics and productivity insights
- To power local, user-controlled automations and assistant workflows

## Data sharing
- No data is sold
- No data is shared with advertisers
- No data is intentionally disclosed to unrelated third parties

## Storage
- Data may be stored locally on the account owner's own devices or private systems for personal use
- Tokens and credentials are intended to be stored in secure local credential stores where possible

## Scope
This integration is not a public consumer app or commercial service. It is for the account owner's personal use only.
